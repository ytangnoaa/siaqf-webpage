ML-Powered Air Quality Forecasting

SIAQF is a SL/ML-based air quality model based on DeepAQM framework designed to provide fast and reliable predictions of atmospheric pollutants, including surface PM2.5 and ozone (O3).

About SIAQF

SIAQF is a deep learning-based air quality modeling system developed to complement traditional physics-based chemical transport models. The framework leverages meteorological information, geographical features, emissions, and air quality observations to predict the spatial and temporal evolution of atmospheric pollutants.

The system builds upon UFS-DeepAQM with convolutional long short-term memory (ConvLSTM) and other deep learning architectures to emulate the behavior of operational air quality models, enabling computationally efficient air quality predictions.

Model Capabilities

PM2.5 Forecasting

Hourly surface PM2.5 predictions with forecast lead times of up to 72 hours.

Ozone Forecasting

High-resolution surface ozone predictions using meteorological and atmospheric composition inputs.

Data Fusion

Integration of air quality observations and model information to improve prediction accuracy.

SI/ML Model Development

Development of advanced deep learning architectures for atmospheric composition prediction and emulation.
